TV Launcher

Changes appear on the TV straight away.

Launcher

Automatic

Autopilot picks the launcher by itself when the home screen comes back or ambient mode starts: one by day, another after sunset, another on red days. Choosing a launcher by hand wins until the next change. Evening light warms and dims the screen after sunset. At bedtime, if nobody's using the TV, it fades to black over ten minutes. Weather on Photos: rain on the glass, snow, frost below 0°, and holiday sparks.

People & notes

Shown under the clock on the Photos launcher. Name days and birthdays appear the day before ("Tomorrow: Dad's name day") and on the day. Weekly and monthly notes also appear the day before and on the day. In ambient mode the Monkey Island pirate reminds you of today's.

Ambient mode

After this long without the remote, the launcher puts its buttons away and shows only its picture and a small clock (on Monkey Island, a pirate takes a walk). Any button brings it back.

Apps

    The name is what the launchers show. Monkey Island's font has letters, digits and + - ! : . ' only.

    Photos

    For the Photos launcher, a new one every 45 seconds. New photos are shown first, with "from" and your name. About once an hour it shows one taken this week in an earlier year. Otherwise it favours photos that suit the moment: the same time of year, snowy ones when it snows, warm ones in the evening. Landscape photos look best. With none uploaded it shows its built-in photos.

    Monkey Island backgrounds

    Shown in name order, changing every 5 minutes. Pixel art stays sharp; scenes of about 320×144 fit best. Ambient mode's pirate walks through these too, with his feet 90% of the way down; to choose the row, end the name with _g and the row number, e.g. dock_g130.png.

    Monkey Island ambient walker

    Replaces the built-in pirate in ambient mode. One row of equal walking frames, facing right, at the game's own pixel size (about 30–80 px tall; bigger sheets are shrunk). A flat background colour is made transparent. Takes effect the next time ambient mode starts.

    Update the launcher

    First, the TV has to allow the launcher to install updates. This is needed once.

    On the TV, turn on Minimal TV Launcher ("Allow from this source"), then press Back.

    Download the new MinimalTVLauncher.apk to this phone, then choose it here. The TV asks before installing; keep it on the home screen.
