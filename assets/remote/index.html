Ambient mode

After this long without the remote, the launcher puts its buttons away and shows only its picture and a small clock (on Monkey Island, a pirate takes a walk). Any button brings it back.

Apps

    The name is what the launchers show. Monkey Island's font has letters, digits and + - ! : . ' only.

    Photos

    For the Photos launcher, shown in name order, a new one every 45 seconds. Landscape photos look best. With none uploaded it shows its built-in photos of Riga.

    Monkey Island backgrounds

    Shown in name order, changing every 5 minutes. Pixel art stays sharp; scenes of about 320×144 fit best.

    Update the launcher

    First, the TV has to allow the launcher to install updates. This is needed once.

    On the TV, turn on Minimal TV Launcher ("Allow from this source"), then press Back.

    Download the new MinimalTVLauncher.apk to this phone, then choose it here. The TV asks before installing; keep it on the home screen.
